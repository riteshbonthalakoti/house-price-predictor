Machine Learning & The AI Landscape

An introduction to core concepts, model types, and real-world applications

What is Machine Learning?

"Learning patterns from data, without being explicitly programmed with rules"

Supervised
Model learns from labeled data with inputs and known target outputs
Unsupervised
Model discovers hidden patterns and natural clusters in unlabeled data
Reinforcement
Model learns optimal actions through trial, error, and feedback rewards

Evolution of AI Capabilities

Predictive AI
Forecasts numerical outcomes or categories based on historical data
Example: Spam filter / House prices
Generative AI
Creates brand new content like text, code, images, or audio
Example: ChatGPT writing text
Agentic AI
Autonomous systems that plan and execute multi-step real actions
Example: AI booking a flight for you
"Predictive AI answers a question. Generative AI creates something new. Agentic AI goes and does something in the world."

Linear Regression — The 4 Ingredients

"Calculating a final estimated number by adding up the value of every input detail"

1. The Details
Inputs
The facts you give the model (like size, age, or location rating).
2. Impact
Importance
How much money each detail adds (+) or subtracts (-) from the total.
3. Base
Starting Price
The minimum baseline value before adding any specific details.
4. Answer
Final Estimate
The final predicted number the model calculates for you.
💡 Plain English Idea: Final Estimate = Starting Price + (Detail 1 × Impact) + (Detail 2 × Impact)

How The Model Learns & Tests Itself

1. How It Learns (Practice)
Looking at Examples
The computer studies hundreds of past examples where the real answer is already known.
Reducing Errors
It keeps adjusting the importance of each detail until its guesses are as close to reality as possible.
2. How We Test It (Accuracy Check)
Checking New Examples
We test the model on brand new data it has never seen before to make sure it didn't just memorize.
Scoring Accuracy
We measure how far off its guesses were on average to calculate an overall accuracy score.
